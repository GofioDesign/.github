# Gofio Design

**Cultivamos soluciones** · *We grow solutions*

Proyectos y consultoría para la transformación ecosocial. Tecnología, diseño, agricultura y talleres, desde Tenerife.

Projects and consultancy for eco-social change. Technology, design, farming and workshops, from Tenerife, Canary Islands, since 2005.

---

### What we do

Technology is one of our tools, not the goal. We run our own projects and we also work alongside public bodies, nature reserves, schools, companies and neighbourhood communities.

- **Digital identity and infrastructure:** brand systems, light static websites, data networks.
- **Canarian landscape and territory:** gardens with native flora, rewilding.
- **Asset management and community engagement:** heritage interpretation, environmental education, energy-efficiency workshops, sustainable events.

### How we work

- **Problem · Idea · Plan · Done.** Every project is explained in those four steps.
- **Open and shared.** Open source whenever we can, so others can reuse and federate the work.
- **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.2 AA is part of the identity, not a final checkbox.
- **Autonomy.** We leave clients able to carry on by themselves: their tools, their data, their content.

### Why «Gofio»

Gofio is the heart of Canarian heritage: a flour made from toasted, ground grain. Grow, toast, grind, share. That is how we like to work: we start from what is local and turn it, patiently, into something that feeds the community.
